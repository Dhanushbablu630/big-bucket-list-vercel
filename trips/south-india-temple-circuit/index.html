PILGRIMAGE DETAILS

Temples on this journey

  • Srikalahasti — Shiva temple associated with Vayu (air)
  • Tiruvannamalai — Arunachaleswarar temple associated with Agni (fire)
  • Chidambaram — Nataraja temple associated with Akasha (space)
  • Kanchipuram — Ekambareswarar temple associated with Prithvi (earth)
  • A complete Pancha Bhoota circuit also includes Jambukeswarar at Tiruvanaikaval, associated with water; confirm whether it is part of the selected package.
